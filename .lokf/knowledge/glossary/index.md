# Glossary

* [Collection](collection.md) - A collection in the Collections Ontology (`coll:Collection`): a group of things taken as a whole, with its elements and its size; a set holds each element once, and a bag may hold one more than once.
* [Bag](bag.md) - A bag in the Collections Ontology (`coll:Bag`): a collection that may hold one element more than once, in no order, through items that each wrap one element.
* [List](list.md) - A list in the Collections Ontology (`coll:List`): a bag whose items are in order, each with its place from 1, a first and a last item, and links to the items before and after it.
* [Item](item.md) - An item in the Collections Ontology (`coll:Item`, the schema's `CoItem`): an entry of a bag that wraps one of the bag's elements in `itemContent`.
